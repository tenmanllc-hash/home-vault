01What HomeVault does

Store any secret

Passwords, cards, secure notes, Wi-Fi codes, software keys, documents and images — all encrypted. Add custom fields for anything else.

Autofill

Plugs into Android's Autofill service to fill usernames and passwords into websites and apps, and can offer to save new logins as you create them.

Backup & sync

Export your vault as one encrypted backup file, or keep several devices in step through your own Google Drive — end-to-end encrypted, merged record by record.

Security you can trust

XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption with Argon2 key derivation, screenshot and app-switcher protection, biometric unlock, and auto-lock.

03Security & recovery

How secure is it if my phone is lost or stolen?

Your vault is encrypted at rest and locked behind your master password (and biometrics). Nobody can read your secrets without that password, even someone holding the device.

What if my Google account is compromised?

Cloud sync is end-to-end encrypted. Anyone reaching your Drive finds only an encrypted file they can't open without your master password, which never leaves your device.

How limited and safe is HomeVault's access to my Google Drive?

Very limited. HomeVault requests the narrowest Google Drive permission available, called drive.file. It can see and manage only the files and folders HomeVault itself created — never your documents, photos, or anything else in your Drive.

The files it does create are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded, so Google only stores an unreadable blob. You can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time, and revoke the permission at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

I forgot my master password — can it be recovered?

No. If you forget your master password, there is no way to recover your vault. HomeVault is zero-knowledge: there is no backdoor, no reset link and no recovery email. This is what keeps your data private, so choose a password you'll remember.

Warning: if you forget your master password, your vault is lost for good — we can't recover it, and neither can Google. Choose a password you will remember, and keep a backup file somewhere safe.

04Autofill

How does Autofill decide which login to offer?

The Android Autofill service matches against the Title and Keywords fields of your Login items. For a login to show up on a website, make sure its Title or Keywords contain the site's domain, for example example.com.

How do I use Autofill in Chrome on Android?

First choose HomeVault as your Autofill service in Android settings. Then, in Chrome, open Settings and turn on Autofill using another service. Without this, Chrome keeps using its own password manager instead of HomeVault.

The Autofill suggestion isn't showing up. What can I do?

Tap out of the username or password field and tap back into it again to re-focus it, or refresh the page. The suggestion usually appears after that.

05Free & Premium

Are there any limits?

The free version never expires: up to 15 items, with full encryption, autofill and biometric unlock. A Premium subscription unlocks unlimited items, encrypted Google Drive sync, and local backups. Your existing items always stay readable and editable, even if a subscription lapses.

There is no trial and no account. Premium is a yearly subscription billed through Google Play.

06Vaults & sync

Can I share a vault with family?

Yes — create a dedicated vault, sync it to a shared Google account, and share its master password with trusted family members. Everyone who opens it sees the same encrypted data. To keep it safe, set an optional edit PIN: it gates adding, editing, and deleting, preventing accidental changes and keeping family members without the PIN to view-only access. The PIN travels with the vault, staying in effect on every synced device.

Multiple vaults (personal, work, family)?

Yes. Create and name multiple vaults; the device works with one at a time and you switch between them with Open Vault.

How do I switch to a vault on a different Google account?

Yes, vaults on different Google accounts are supported. To switch:

  1. Go to Settings and tap Disconnect Cloud to sign out of the current Google Drive account.
  2. Tap Open Vault, then enter the name of the vault folder in Google Drive and its password.
  3. When asked to select a Google account, choose the one that holds that vault.

After that, Sync with Cloud works with the vault tied to that account.

Local backup vs Google Drive sync?

Local backup keeps a private encrypted file on your device — fully offline, but only on that phone. Drive sync keeps an end-to-end-encrypted copy in the cloud, so you can restore or sync across devices. Both are encrypted; Drive adds cross-device convenience.
